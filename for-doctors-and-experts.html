For doctors, consultants and experts

Be known for your judgement, within your profession's rules.

Peers refer to you, patients and clients hear of you, and a search may be the first thing anyone does. We help what they find be accurate and calm.

Rise Personal · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

The short answer

Rise Personal helps doctors, consultants and other experts build a clear professional reputation within the advertising and conduct rules of their licensing body or professional association. We use your own words, avoid patient stories and before and after images, and you approve every piece. The final responsibility for compliance stays with you.

Reputation without advertising

Experts rarely want to advertise. Doctors in particular work under rules that limit how a practice can promote itself. At the same time, referrals, hospital appointments, speaking invitations and public trust all rely on people knowing who you are and what you stand for.

Our work sits in that space: a clear, accurate account of your expertise and values, visible to the people who need to find it, and never loud.

Working within your licensing body's rules

Rules differ by country and often by region. Bodies such as the Dubai Health Authority, the Department of Health Abu Dhabi, the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties or the General Medical Council each set their own. Other professions have their own codes. These rules generally cover claims about results, comparisons with colleagues, testimonials and the use of patient images. You know the rules that apply to you better than we do.

So we work within them. If you give us the rules that apply to you, we will check each piece against them on request before it goes live. You approve everything, and the final responsibility for compliance stays with you.

  • No patient stories and no patient testimonials
  • No before and after images
  • No claims of being the best, and no comparisons with other doctors
  • No health promises, cures or guaranteed outcomes
  • Please do not send us patient information, even by WhatsApp

What we do build

Within those limits, there is a good deal to build.

  • In the room: how you are introduced to colleagues, referrers, hospital boards and conference organisers
  • On the record: a professional profile, articles and explainers in your own words, and accurate mentions in the press
  • On screen: a restrained, factual presence, such as a clear professional page or a considered talk

Consultants and other experts

Consultants, lawyers, engineers, academics and advisers face a gentler version of the same question. Your work travels by introduction. Your clients cannot name them. You are better than the way you are described.

We help you explain what you do in plain words, choose the few subjects where you want to be quoted, and be present in the rooms where your next clients and peers already are. Confidentiality applies in both directions. We never ask you to reveal a client.

Your name, your voice

We write from your own words and notes, in your voice, so that what appears sounds like a thoughtful colleague and not like a brochure. We use plain language and avoid superlatives. If a sentence could mislead a patient or a client, we cut it.

A calm pace

Expert work is demanding. We plan a pace you can keep next to a clinic, a case load or a practice. Fewer, better pieces, approved by you, serve you better than a burst of activity. Our page on wellness and personal branding explains how we think about pace.

Confidential from the first message

Every engagement starts under an NDA, signed before you describe the brief. We never name a client, quote you or show your work without your written approval.

How to start

Message our team with your profession and the country or region where you are licensed. Please do not include patient details. We listen first, then confirm scope, timing and price in writing before you commit.

Questions people ask us

Do you work with doctors?

Yes. We build a clear reputation within the advertising and conduct rules of your licensing body. We do not use patient stories, patient testimonials or before and after images.

Will you check my content against my regulator's rules?

We work within them. On request, and with the rules you give us, we check each piece against them before it goes live. You approve everything, and the final responsibility for compliance stays with you.

Can I post as myself rather than as my clinic?

Often yes, though rules sometimes differ for individuals and facilities. Check with your licensing body. We can plan around either.

I am a lawyer, engineer or adviser. Is this for me?

Yes. Any expert whose work travels by trust can use it. Your professional code applies, and we work within it.

Do you share my details with a hospital or an employer?

No. We share nothing about you without your written approval.

Can you promise more patients or clients?

No. We do not promise referrals, followers, rankings or revenue. We help you be clearly and accurately known.
