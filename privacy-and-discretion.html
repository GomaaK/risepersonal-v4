Privacy and discretion

How we protect your information and your name.

This page describes how the site and our team handle information today, in plain words. If anything changes, we will say so here first.

Rise Personal · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

The short answer

Today the Rise Personal website sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts or fonts, and the Brand DNA Report runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to us. If you message us on WhatsApp or email, our team sees those messages. Client work is confidential: we sign an NDA before we hear the brief.

What we collect today

The website itself does not collect your name, your email address or your answers. It has no sign-up form, no analytics and no advertising tools. It sets no cookies and loads no scripts or fonts from other companies. The fonts are served from our own site.

Like any website, the service that hosts it handles the technical request your browser makes in order to show you a page, which includes your IP address. We do not add tracking on top of that, and we do not use it to identify you.

The only information we receive is what you choose to send us, by WhatsApp or email.

What stays on your device

Some features keep small pieces of information in your browser's own storage on your device. This is not a cookie and it is not sent to us. The role you pick on the home page is kept for the length of your visit. The Brand DNA Report keeps your progress and your result on your device so that you can pick up where you left off.

You can remove this at any time by clearing the site data for risepersonal.com in your browser settings.

The Brand DNA Report

The report works out your archetype in your browser. Your answers, your first name if you enter one, and your result are not sent to us. The PDF and the share card are made on your device and saved to your device.

If you choose to message us about your result, for example by pressing a WhatsApp button, that message is sent through WhatsApp and our team will see whatever it contains. Look at the message before you send it. You can edit it.

Who sees your messages

If you message us on WhatsApp or write to Authentic@risepersonal.com, our team sees those messages. We do not sell them and we do not share them outside the team. We share them inside the team only as far as the work needs.

Before an NDA is in place, please keep your first message short: your role and what you would like to talk about. Please do not send patient information, passwords, identity documents or financial account details.

How client confidentiality works

Every engagement starts under an NDA, signed before we hear the brief. We never name a client, quote them or show their work without their written approval. Nothing is made public in your name without your written approval.

When we describe our work on this site, we do so without names or identifying detail, and we share more only where a client has agreed.

How long we keep messages

We keep messages only as long as needed to reply and to work together, and we delete them if you ask.

How to ask us to delete your messages

Send a message on WhatsApp or an email to Authentic@risepersonal.com saying that you would like your messages deleted. We will delete what we hold and tell you when it is done. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

Messages that remain in your own WhatsApp or email account are yours to remove.

Third parties

The site loads nothing from third parties. WhatsApp and email are third-party services that you choose to use when you contact us, and they have their own privacy terms. The site also links to the websites of companies in our ecosystem. Those sites have their own rules, which apply once you leave this one.

If this changes

We plan to add optional features later, such as an email sign-up to receive your report and simple visit statistics. If we do, we will update this page before they go live. We will say what is collected, why, who sees it and how to say no, and we will ask for your consent where it is needed. Nothing will be switched on before this page says so.

Contact

Questions about this page or about your information: WhatsApp us at +971 50 683 2835 or write to Authentic@risepersonal.com.

Questions people ask us

Does the website use cookies?

No. It sets no cookies. A few features keep small items in your browser storage on your device, as described above, and they are not sent to us.

Does the Brand DNA Report send my answers to you?

No. It runs in your browser. We receive something only if you choose to send it to us yourself.

Do you use Google Analytics or social media pixels?

No. The site loads no analytics, advertising or social media scripts.

Who can read my WhatsApp message?

Our team. WhatsApp itself is a third-party service with its own terms.

Can you delete my messages?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp or by email and we will delete them and tell you when it is done.

Will this page change?

Yes, when we add anything new. We will update it before the change goes live.
