Leadership Visibility Check for organisations

The short answer

Leadership visibility is whether the people who run an organisation are known, introduced consistently and heard in public in a way that fits each of them. This check does not compare you with other organisations. It shows where your own leaders are visible, where they are not, and what to do first.
