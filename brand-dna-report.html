What the Brand DNA Report measures

The Brand DNA Report reads five traits from your answers: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness and emotional steadiness. It then matches your pattern to one of twelve brand archetypes and builds your three words from the archetype and your own choices.

Why start here

A personal brand works when it is built on who you are, not on a template. The Brand DNA Report gives you a clear starting point, and it is the first step of how we work with founders, executives and experts across Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the GCC.

Questions

What is the Brand DNA Report?

A free quiz from Rise Personal. It uses twenty statements based on the Big Five personality traits and two choices about how you want to be perceived. It matches your pattern to one of twelve brand archetypes and builds your three words.

How long does it take?

About four minutes.

Are my answers stored?

Not by us. Your answers and result stay in your browser, saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. We receive something only if you choose to send it. You can clear it by removing the site data for this website in your browser settings.

What do I get?

A six-page branded PDF: your primary and secondary archetype, your three words, five trait scores, what to work on, a wellness pairing, a note from the founder and a seven-day plan. You also get a card to share.

Is it a clinical or hiring test?

No. It is a positioning tool and a starting point for a conversation about your personal brand. It is not a medical, psychological or recruitment assessment.

What happens if I message you my result?

The message goes through WhatsApp and our team sees what it contains. You can read and edit it before you send. If you do not send it, we never see your result.

What is the wellness pairing?

A practice that suits your archetype, such as rest and boundaries or discipline and fitness. It is a suggestion for reflection, not medical advice.
