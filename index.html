Our approach

Online gets you seen. The room gets you trusted.

We start on the ground and let online amplify it. Choose a stage.

Be the person people already want to meet.

We look at the rooms you walk into: boardrooms, conferences, investor meetings, introductions. We shape how you are presented and remembered there.

  • Positioning built from who you are
  • Speaking, panels and introductions
  • A clear story people repeat for you

Be quoted, listed and referenced.

Once the story is clear, we put it on the record: press, interviews, profiles and credible mentions that a search or an investor will find.

  • Media and thought-leadership plan
  • Profiles and features
  • Reputation that survives a Google search

Show up online as the same person.

LinkedIn, video and content that sound like you, not like an agency. Online follows the reputation you earn in the room, so it stays consistent and human.

  • Content and scripts in your voice
  • Video, short-form and social
  • Distribution and paid reach

Doing it before it had a name.

As a child, Sawaf already noticed what made a person stand out, and he helped people be seen for it. He did not know it was personal branding. It was simply what he loved doing.

A sculptor who creates from scratch.

Sawaf graduated from the sculpture department and worked as an interior engineer. His selling point was sculpture: the ability to imagine something and create it from nothing. Before he changed careers, he also made many statues and sculptures across Egypt.

Shaping something raw until people stop and look taught him a lasting lesson. The same eye for form and presence now goes into how people are seen.

Learning how business really moves.

He moved into marketing and business development. He worked with public-sector clients through Bnsights, and on large private-sector projects for high-end clients. He learned how companies win trust, how deals are made, and why people buy from people long before they buy from brands.

He never did it alone. He and his partners complete the puzzle together, each bringing what the others lack.

The pattern had a name.

Then it became clear: what he had always done was personal branding. And it was impressive what it could do. Supporting people to unleash their potential changed their position and their income. He turned his passion into his work.

From a talent to a method.

He learned from the experts, then tested what he learned with real clients, again and again. What began as passion and talent became a story and a process: frameworks that were tried many times, and they worked.

Questions

Before you message us.

What does Rise Personal do?

We help founders, executives, doctors and consultants become known and trusted by the right people. We start in the rooms where reputation is built: introductions, boards, speaking and investor meetings. Then we put it on the record and online, in your own voice.

Where do you work and in which languages?

We are based in Dubai and work remotely with clients in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the wider Gulf, Egypt and Europe. We do not have an office in Saudi Arabia or Egypt. We work by video call, WhatsApp and email, in English today. Arabic is planned.

How do I start and what does it cost?

Start with the free Brand DNA Report, message our team on WhatsApp, or ask for a private 20-minute call. We price by scope of work, not by likes or followers. We confirm scope, timing and price in writing before you commit. We do not promise followers, rankings or revenue.

Is my information safe?

We sign an NDA before we hear your brief. We never name a client, quote you or show your work without your written approval. The website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. The Brand DNA Report runs in your browser and sends nothing to us. If you message us on WhatsApp or email, our team sees those messages, and we delete them if you ask. Our privacy and discretion page has the detail.

What is the Brand DNA Report?

A free quiz of 22 taps, about four minutes. It gives you your brand archetype, a secondary archetype, your three words, five trait scores, what to work on, a note from the founder and a seven-day plan, in a six-page PDF. No sign-up is needed and your answers stay in your browser. It is a positioning tool, not a clinical or hiring test.

How long before I see results?

Some changes are quick to see, such as a clearer story, a rewritten profile and a prepared introduction. Reputation itself grows more slowly and depends on how often you are in the right rooms. We give you a timing estimate in writing before you commit. We do not promise followers, rankings or revenue.

Will you post or write in my name?

Only with your approval. You see and approve every public piece before it goes live. If you want us to write for you, we write in your voice, from your own words and notes.

What if we are not the right fit?

We will say so in the first conversation or later in the process. We would rather tell you than take on work we cannot do well.

Do you work with doctors?

Yes. We build a clear reputation within the advertising and conduct rules of your licensing body. We do not use patient stories or before and after images. On request, we check each piece against the rules you give us, you approve everything, and the final responsibility for compliance stays with you.

What is human-powered marketing?

A marketing approach built on people rather than logos or products. People trust people, so a company's most reliable brand is the visible reputation of the people who lead it and speak for it.

Is wellness a health service at Rise Personal?

No. Wellness here is a lens and a practice for leaders: a pace of visibility you can sustain, with boundaries and rest planned in. We do not give medical advice, offer therapy or make health claims.
