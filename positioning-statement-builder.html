Positioning Statement Builder

The short answer

A positioning statement says who something is for, what they need, what you are, the main benefit, what it is different from and why that difference is believable. It is an internal tool first. You use it to decide what to say, then you say it in your own words.

An example

An invented example, not a real company, to show the shape.

For independent clinics with a small admin team who lose appointments because phone bookings are hard to manage, we are a scheduling service that fills gaps in the day and sends reminders so fewer slots stay empty. Unlike paper diaries and general-purpose booking tools, we are different because it is built around how small clinics actually run, by people who have worked in one.
