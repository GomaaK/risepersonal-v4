Egypt

In Egypt, a good name travels by word of mouth.

It reaches people long before a brochure does. We help make sure what travels is true, clear and yours.

Rise Personal · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

The short answer

Rise Personal is a personal branding agency based in Dubai that works remotely with company leaders, founders, doctors and consultants in Egypt. We make a reputation that already travels by word of mouth clear and consistent, in the room first and then online. We have no office in Egypt and work in English today, with Arabic planned.

Reputation travels person to person

In Egypt, clients, partners and talent ask who is behind a company, and they ask people they trust. The answer they receive shapes the decision before any presentation does.

A personal brand gives that conversation something true and consistent to point to. It does not replace word of mouth. It gives word of mouth better material.

Cairo, Alexandria and across Egypt

Cairo and Alexandria are different cities, and a reputation often has to hold in both. We work with clients across Egypt, remotely, through video sessions, message workshops and written reviews. Everything is documented so that your team keeps the work.

Founders, experts and company leaders

A founder who is known in their circle but not beyond it. A consultant whose work travels only by introduction. A company leader whose team is stronger than the way the company is described. These are the people we work with.

For doctors and other regulated professionals, we work within the rules of the licensing body, such as the Egyptian Medical Syndicate for physicians. We avoid patient stories and before and after images. You approve everything.

Egypt and the Gulf

Many Egyptian professionals have clients, employers or ambitions in the Gulf as well as at home. Your story may need to work for both audiences. We help you decide what stays the same in both places and what changes: the rooms, the tone and the channels.

We are based in Dubai, so we see both sides of that conversation.

A founder who knows Egypt

Our founder, Mohamed El Sawaf, graduated from a sculpture department and worked as an interior engineer. Before he changed careers he made many statues and sculptures across Egypt. He then moved into marketing and business development, including public-sector work through Bnsights, and has focused on personal branding since 2023. Our ecosystem also includes La7ma, an online fresh-meat store in Egypt.

Arabic, time zones and ways to talk

Our site and our work are in English today. Arabic is planned. If your audience needs Arabic content now, tell us at the start and we will say plainly whether we can meet it.

We do not have an office in Egypt. Egypt is one to two hours behind Dubai, depending on the season. We meet by video, WhatsApp and email, and agree times that suit your week.

What working with us looks like

We start by listening. Then we write down your voice and values in a blueprint you approve. Then we build the message, identity and reach you need. Every stage is explained on our how we work page, and nothing goes public without your written approval.

A practical way to start

Start small if you prefer. The free Brand DNA Report takes about four minutes. Then talk to us about your goals, and we confirm scope, timing and price in writing before you commit.

Questions people ask us

Do you have an office in Egypt?

No. We are based in Dubai and work with clients in Egypt remotely.

Do you work with companies or only individuals?

Both. Our main audience is leaders and the companies they lead. Trust attaches to people, so a company's most reliable brand is often the visible reputation of the people behind it.

Can you help me be known in the Gulf as well as in Egypt?

We can help you shape a story that works for both audiences and choose the rooms that fit. We do not promise specific introductions or deals.

Do you work in Arabic?

Not yet. We work in English today, and Arabic is planned. Tell us at the start if you need Arabic.

What does a personal branding consultant do?

We help you find what makes you distinctive and turn it into clear positioning, message and visual identity. We start with listening and your values, then move to how and where you are seen.
