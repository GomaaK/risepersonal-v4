The Sage

The thought leader and teacher: people come to you to understand what things mean.

Insightful · Grounded · Wise

The short answer

You are trusted for how you think. People come to you to understand what something means, not only what to do.

In the room

You do your best work in a small group where someone asks the hard question. Prepare one clear idea per meeting and let others reach it with you.

On the record

Publish the thinking behind your decisions: a short essay, a considered quote, a talk with a clear argument. Choose depth over frequency, and make each piece easy to cite.

On screen

Write long enough to teach and short enough to finish. A calm, plain voice on video suits you better than energy.

What to work on

Sharing your ideas publicly: the world needs your thinking out loud.

Waiting until your thinking is complete before sharing it. A Sage who stays private can look like someone with nothing to say.

Wellness pairing

Deep work & meditation. Deep work and meditation keep your clarity sharp: a Sage’s brand only holds when the mind behind it is rested.

Three questions to ask yourself

  • What do people already ask you to explain?
  • Which one idea would you stand behind in public?
  • What are you waiting to be certain about?
