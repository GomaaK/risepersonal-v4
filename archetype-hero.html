Archetype 04 of 12

The Hero

The achiever: proof, momentum and results follow you.

Resolute · Proven · Bold

The short answer

You are known for proof. People remember what you did when it was hard.

In the room

Tell the story of a difficult moment plainly, including what it cost. Specific facts make the account credible and need no decoration.

On the record

Let your record speak: projects delivered, problems solved, commitments kept. Where it is appropriate, ask people who were there to describe it, with their permission.

On screen

Show the work in progress as well as the finish. Steady posts about the discipline behind a result suit you better than announcements.

What to work on

Sustainable pace: a long campaign beats a brilliant sprint.

Treating every conversation as a contest. A reputation built only on winning makes people careful around you.

Wellness pairing

Discipline & fitness. Discipline and fitness keep the engine honest: your brand promises proof, so train like it.

Three questions to ask yourself

  • What is the hardest thing you finished?
  • Who helped, and do they know you say so?
  • What do you want to be known for besides winning?
