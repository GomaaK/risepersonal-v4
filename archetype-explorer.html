The Explorer

The founder-nomad: new territory is your natural habitat.

Free · Open · Untamed

The short answer

You are known for going first. People follow you into places they have not been.

In the room

Bring one story from a place or project others have not seen, and connect it to a problem in the room. Curiosity lands best when it ends in something useful.

On the record

Write about what you found, including what did not work. A clear thread through your moves, markets or fields gives readers a reason to follow.

On screen

Share the work in short, honest pieces from where you are. Keep one home base, a profile or a page, so people know where to find you.

What to work on

Rooted storytelling: bring the journey home so people can follow it.

Moving on before anyone can follow. Constant change can look like a lack of commitment to partners who want to rely on you.

Wellness pairing

Nature & movement. Nature and movement reset your compass: the Explorer’s brand stays true only at full breath.

Three questions to ask yourself

  • What is the thread through everything you have tried?
  • Where is your home base online?
  • What have you learned that others could use?
