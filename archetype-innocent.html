The Innocent

The optimistic minimalist: clarity and honesty are your aesthetic.

Honest · Calm · Clear

The short answer

You are known for clarity and honesty. People find calm in how you put things.

In the room

Say the simple thing, plainly, and let the silence sit. Your steadiness helps others slow down and decide.

On the record

State your values and keep to them in public. A short, clear position that does not change from one setting to the next builds trust over time.

On screen

A clean, minimal profile and a few clear posts suit you. Raise your voice a little, because a quiet message still has to be heard.

What to work on

A bolder voice: your calm deserves volume, too.

Being read as naive. Back your honesty with specifics so that it reads as experience.

Wellness pairing

Mindfulness & simplicity. Mindfulness and simplicity are your competitive edge: in a noisy market, your calm is the brand.

Three questions to ask yourself

  • What do you believe that you rarely say?
  • Where could a little more volume help?
  • Which specifics show your experience?
