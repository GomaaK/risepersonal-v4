Archetype 02 of 12

The Creator

The artist and builder: you exist to make things that didn’t exist before.

Original · Visionary · Crafted

The short answer

You are known for making things that did not exist before. Your work is the argument.

In the room

Bring the work, not a description of it. A sketch, a prototype or a single page on the table does more than a pitch.

On the record

Show how the work gets made and credit the people who help you make it. Finished pieces with a short account of the decision behind them are easy for others to quote.

On screen

Let the visuals carry your profile: one consistent style, one clear format, a regular rhythm. Short behind-the-scenes clips suit you if you keep them honest.

What to work on

Finishing and shipping: done is the masterpiece your ideas are waiting for.

Starting new things before the last one has been seen. Projects that never ship leave nothing for a reputation to rest on.

Wellness pairing

Flow states. Flow states are your strategy: your best brand moves arrive when the making feels like play.

Three questions to ask yourself

  • What have you made that people still mention?
  • What is finished but not yet shown?
  • Who should see it first?
